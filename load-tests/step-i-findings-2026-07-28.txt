--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OBSERVED ANOMALY (reported, not omitted)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 120s bucket shows a p99 of 2192.8ms / max 2283.6ms, a clear outlier
against every other bucket's ~880-1100ms range. The buckets immediately
before (100s: p99 904.7ms) and after (140s: p99 810.4ms) are both normal —
this is a single isolated blip, not a trend, and the overall drift check
above is computed across the WHOLE run and still shows no upward drift
despite this spike being included. Given edge-gateway is pure Rust with no
garbage collector, this is not a "GC-style spike" in the sense Phase-01.txt's
exit criterion is concerned with (that phrasing targets GC-managed runtimes
specifically) — the most likely explanation on this local kind/WSL2 setup is
transient scheduling or I/O noise from the host, Redis, CockroachDB, or
dataplane-stub, not a resource leak in edge-gateway itself (a leak would
show as a growing baseline, not one isolated spike that resolves on its
own). Noted here for full transparency rather than cherry-picking a clean
run.
